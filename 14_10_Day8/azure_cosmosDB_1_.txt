Section A – Basic Select  

 

Display all documents in the container. 

SELECT * FROM c 

 

Display only guest_name , city , and hotel_name . 

 

 SELECT c.guest_name, c.city, c.hotel_name from c 

 

Display guest_name , room_type , nights , and booking_amount 

 

SELECT c.guest_name, c.room_type, c.nights, c.booking_amount from c 

 

Display id , guest_name , and booking_status . 

 

SELECT c.id, c.guest_name, c.booking_status from c
